## Claude-only notes
- Subagents in `.claude/agents/` are thin wrappers around `.agents/roles/*`. Use the `explorer` subagent for read-only searches so your main context stays clean.
- Executor subagents `executor-<tier>` and `.claude/skills/` are GENERATED by `python scripts/agent/sync.py` from `.agents/config.json` and `.agents/skills/`. Do not edit them directly.
